01 // THE SACRED LORE

Every mountain flame carries a homecoming story.

Why do the hills of Uttarakhand light their lamps eleven days after Diwali, on Kartik Shukla Ekadashi? Oral folk songs and village memory keep two beloved accounts alive.

✦ TRADITIONAL GARHWALI BHAILO CHANT
“भैलो रे भैलो, काखड़ी को रैलू,
उज्यालो होलो पहाड़ मा, ओ भैलो रे भैलो!
बारह बग्वाल मा एक इगास,
सुख-समृद्धि को सदा उजास!”

“Bhailo re Bhailo, kakhadi ko railu — Ujyalo holo pahad ma, O Bhailo re Bhailo!”

As villagers gather in the stone courtyard (kholi) and whirl flaming bundles of resinous pine and Bhimal wood, this rhythmic call invites light, harvest abundance, and fearless togetherness across the high ridges.

✎ echoed across every Garhwali kholi on Kartik Ekadashi!
FOLKLORE // 01

Madho Singh Bhandari’s Return

In a cherished Garhwali ballad, General Madho Singh Bhandari led King Mahipat Shah’s troops on a winter campaign toward the Tibetan frontier (Dapaghati). With soldiers still away on Amavasya, villages refused to celebrate Diwali. When the warriors returned victorious 11 days later, the hills erupted with diyas and whirling Bhailo torches.

FOLKLORE // 02

When News Crossed the High Passes

Another oral tradition holds that news of Lord Rama’s return to Ayodhya took eleven extra days to traverse the steep Himalayan trails. When the message finally reached remote mountain hamlets on Kartik Ekadashi, families lit their lamps in joyous celebration.

02 // CORE RITUALS OF IGAS

Fire, gratitude & Dhol-Damau beats.

More than a quiet row of lamps, Igas-Bagwal is a kinetic, whole-village celebration honoring nature, livestock, and community.

✎ from morning Go-Puja to midnight fire circles ↗
🔥
NIGHTFALL // RITUAL 01

Bhailo Khelna

भैलो खेलना

Bundles of fragrant pinewood (Chilku) and Bhimal bark rope are bound tightly, lit ablaze, and swung overhead in radiant fiery halos. The whirling sparks symbolize warding off winter gloom and welcoming collective courage.

🌾
MORNING // RITUAL 02

Pashu Dhan Puja

पशुधन एवं गो-पूजा

Before the household feasts, cattle and oxen—the lifeblood of terraced mountain farming—are bathed, their horns anointed with mustard oil and marigold garlands, and fed warm nutrient-rich Pinda of barley, wheat, and jaggery.

🥁
COURTYARD // RITUAL 03

Aipan, Chhura & Jhumelo

ऐपण, छुड़ा और झुमेलो

Thresholds glow with red-ochre Geru and rice-paste Aipan mandalas. As night deepens, the resonant pulse of Dhol-Damau draws young and old into arm-in-arm Jhumelo, Chanchari, and Pandav Nritya folk dances.

03 // PAHADI BHOJ TIER LIST

Flavours of the Himalayan hearth.

No Igas night is complete without a brass platter piled high with slow-cooked Garhwali delicacies. Tap any dish to reveal its traditional tasting notes.

🏆 GOAT Tier • Festival Icon
🍯

Arsa & Gur Pakwan

अरसा और गुड़ के पकवान

The undisputed crown jewel of Garhwali celebrations. Soaked mountain rice is stone-ground, folded into bubbling caramelized jaggery syrup, and fried to a crisp, lacy golden-brown crust with a melt-in-the-mouth center.

Tasting Profile: Deep smoky molasses sweetness from slow-simmered Gur, scented with fennel seeds and cardamom. Stays fresh for weeks in winter mountain air!
👑 Main Character
🥟

Swunla, Puri & Urad Pakora

स्वॉला, पूरी और उड़द पकोड़ी

Crisp festive stuffed puris and spiced black-gram fritters served piping hot straight from the iron kadhai alongside roasted Bhangjeera or Til (sesame) chutney.

Tasting Profile: Crunchy, peppery, and aromatic with wild Himalayan herbs, balanced by bright citrus-sesame chutney.
🔥 Winter Armor
🍲

Gahat Dal & Mandua

गहत की दाल और मंडुवे की रोटी

Velvety horse-gram dal tempered with wild mountain allium (Jamboo / Faran) paired with earthy finger-millet rotis.

Tasting Profile: Served with a generous dollop of fresh white churned butter (Nauni) and jaggery on the side.
💛 Comfort Bowl
🥣

Jhangora ki Kheer

झंगोरे की खीर

Creamy pudding crafted from indigenous Himalayan barnyard millet, slow-reduced in full-cream village milk with raisins and Chironji nuts.

Tasting Profile: Silky, delicate, and nutty—much lighter than rice kheer with a warm green-cardamom finish.
🌾 Sacred First Share
🌾

Pashu Pinda & Roat

पशुधन पिंडा और रोट

Warm balls of cooked grain, jaggery, and ghee offered respectfully to the household cattle during morning Go-Puja, alongside thick sweet Roat.

Cultural Note: Embodies the Pahadi ethic of Prakriti-Prem—thanking the animals and land before celebrating indoors.
